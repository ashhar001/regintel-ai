Status: Synthetic test document. This is not a real regulation and must not be used as legal or compliance advice.

1. Transaction monitoring
A regulated institution must review alerts generated by the transaction-monitoring system within two business days.

2. Escalation
An alert that remains unexplained after initial review must be escalated to an authorized compliance officer.

3. Audit trail
The institution must retain the alert, reviewer decision, evidence considered, escalation status, and resolution timestamp in an auditable record.
