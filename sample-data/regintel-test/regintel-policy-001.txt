RegIntel Test Regulatory Policy 001

Status: Synthetic test document. This is not a real regulation and must not be used as legal or compliance advice.

1. Customer verification
A regulated institution must verify the identity of a customer before activating a regulated account. The verification record must contain the verification method and completion timestamp.

2. Record retention
Customer verification records must be retained for seven years after the customer relationship ends.

3. Enhanced review
A customer classified as high risk must receive an enhanced review before account activation. The enhanced review must be approved by an authorized compliance officer.

4. Periodic review
Standard-risk customers must be reviewed every twenty-four months. High-risk customers must be reviewed every twelve months.

5. Auditability
The institution must maintain an auditable history of verification decisions, reviewer identity, approval status, and material changes to customer risk classification.
